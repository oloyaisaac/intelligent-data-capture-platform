## What a specialist should return

1. Link the ARC ID to their own checklist/finding IDs and reviewed commit; identify a real owner
   through the project's governance process, without assigning a person in this document.
2. Assess prior mitigations and compatibility limits independently, reproduce relevant failures,
   and document remaining risks and proposed remediation or a permitted exception.
3. Provide reviewer-appropriate tests and operational evidence, including real environments when
   required; synthetic tests are not production verification.
4. Return evidence and status to the appropriate gate chairs. High/critical findings block the
   applicable gate under its rules unless a valid, written and dated exception is permitted and
   actually accepted. Do not invent sign-offs, exceptions or completion dates.

No production action, specialist review or independent architect decision is recorded here.
